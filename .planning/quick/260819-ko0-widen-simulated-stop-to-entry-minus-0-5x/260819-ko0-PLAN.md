<objective>
Apply the 0.5 x ATR minimum-risk floor at the simulated ENTRY price, not just at the signal close. `scanner/targets.py` (quick task 260819-g5h) already floors the published stop so `close - stop >= 0.5 x ATR`. But `scanner/simulate.py:160` computes `risk = entry_px - sig.stop` where entry is the NEXT OPEN, so an adverse overnight gap re-collapses the risk denominator — and the close-side floor makes this worse in one specific way: it pins an entire population at exactly 0.5 x ATR, so any negative gap at all pushes them under.

This CHANGES TRADING LOGIC and is the explicitly approved, data-backed task CLAUDE.md requires before altering stop rules. Approved by István on 2026-08-19 against backtest run `038a385_2021-01-01_20260819_142048` (pullback, sp600, 3,820 qualified+resolved trades).

Purpose: entry-based stop/ATR bottoms out at **0.0028** with **551 trades (14.4%) below 0.5**; max R is **56.0** and 11 trades exceed |R| > 10. Raw mean R is +0.064 but winsorized at +-5R it is **-0.010** — the sign flips, which is the original diagnostic that the measured edge is an artifact. It is already manufacturing false discoveries: the winner/loser analysis surfaced `rsi_entry < 48.2` with holdout mean R +0.580 and a CI excluding zero, which looked shippable. It is not real — 78.1% of that bucket sits pinned exactly at the close-side floor and 38.9% has entry risk below 0.5 x ATR; winsorized at +-2R the same bucket is **-0.197**. The rule selects trades whose R is mismeasured, not trades that are profitable. Until this is fixed, every gate decision informed by that pipeline is built on collapsed denominators.
Output: one import, one `effective_stop` assignment, three switched use sites, a docstring correction, a regression test block, and a CLAUDE.md note. No schema change, no new column, no new flag, no change to `targets.py`.
</objective>

<locked_decisions>
These came from `/gsd-discuss-phase` and are NOT open for re-litigation. Do not offer alternatives, do not tune, do not extend.

- **D-01 — Widen, do not skip.** `effective_stop = min(sig.stop, entry_px - 0.5 x ATR)`. The stop may only move FURTHER from entry, never closer. Chosen over skipping on the evidence: the 551 affected trades are 82.9% losers, but on a corrected risk basis they score **+0.152 mean R against +0.014 for the trades that would be kept**, with top rescaled outcomes clustered at 9.2 / 6.7 / 6.7 / 6.3 / 6.3 rather than one freak. Skipping would remove the losers AND the fat right tail that pays for them. Do NOT tune the 0.5 multiplier.
- **D-02 — It must be a REAL stop change, not just a denominator change.** `sig.stop` drives stop-hit detection, the stop-out exit price, the post-stop shadow, and mae_r/mfe_r — not only the risk denominator. ALL of them must use the widened stop. Computing R against a wider stop while still exiting at the tighter one would produce a fake improvement: the change would look like it worked while measuring something that never happened. **This is the single highest risk in the task.**
- **D-03 — The gap guards are unchanged.** `entry_px <= sig.stop` (`gap_skip_down`, line 149) MUST continue to be evaluated against the ORIGINAL signal stop, before any widening. If the open is at or below the planned stop, the setup is broken and the trade is still skipped. Letting a widened stop rescue a gap-skipped trade into a tradeable one is an unintended scope expansion — the same class of bug the guard-order decision in 260819-g5h existed to prevent. `gap_skip_up` (`entry_px >= sig.target`) is likewise unchanged.
- **D-04 — No schema change, no new column, no new flag.** Recording the adjustment was offered and explicitly declined. Do not bump `schema_version`, do not add columns, do not touch `store_db.py`, and do not smuggle the adjustment in as a `Trade.flags` key — `flags` is persisted, so a new key is the same declined feature wearing a different hat.
- **D-05 — `scanner/targets.py` is NOT touched.** The close-side floor stays exactly as shipped.

Out of scope (do not start): re-running the backtest (the user decides; run `038a385_2021-01-01_20260819_142048` becomes stale on merge), any change to gate logic, scoring, or confidence, any web/UI change.
