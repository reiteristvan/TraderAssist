## Phase Goal

**As a** swing trader, **I want to** see how each signal's industry ranks against every other industry in the same scan run, for both live scans and historical backtests computed without look-ahead bias, **so that** I can tell whether a setup's industry is leading or lagging its peers at the moment of the signal.
