<objective>
Deliver the live-scan vertical slice of industry momentum: compute the 20-day industry ETF momentum (IND-02) and the above/below 50-day MA flag (IND-03) for every signal, persist them in dedicated schema v9 columns (IND-05), and wire the values from `run_scan()` through `journal.write_live_signals` into the DB. Look-ahead safety (IND-06) is inherited on the live path because `ctx.market_data` is already sliced to `as_of` by `get_market_data(end=as_of)`.

Purpose: Give every live signal a durable, correctly-anchored industry momentum reading so downstream display (Phase 3) and winner/loser analysis (Phase 4) have real data to query.
Output: `_industry_strength()` in core.py; schema v9 with 4 nullable industry columns; populated live-scan DB rows.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** swing trader, **I want to** have every signal carry its industry group's 20-day momentum and 50-day MA trend, computed without look-ahead bias and stored durably in the signals table, **so that** I can later judge whether a setup sits in a strong or weak industry.
